# Musical Zoo — targeted correction prompts

**Date:** 2026-09-07

**Mode:** Built-in image generation, identity-preserving edits

**Scope:** Targeted identity corrections across accepted Musical Zoo characters

## Riffin companion, habitat, and stickers

The user-supplied full-art reference restored Riffin's strongest construction: the round cream soundboard is the whole belly, exactly three strings travel horizontally across the body, and the instrument continues into one orange tail that curves upward as the neck and ends in exactly three tuning pegs. The corrected companion and habitat prompts explicitly prohibited vertical torso strings and a straight separate neck. The supplied transparent three-pose reference is preserved directly as the V2 sticker source because it is the desired full-art construction rather than an approximation.

## Ringlet companion, habitat, and stickers

Ringlet's earlier round gold clapper hung visibly between the feet and could produce an unintended anatomical reading. The correction keeps the periwinkle rabbit, uneven listening ears, and single flared cream bell torso, but moves one tiny flat rectangular/trapezoidal clapper high inside the dark bell opening. Every format explicitly prohibits a round, oval, pear-shaped, or dangling clapper and keeps the two rabbit feet clearly separated outside the bell silhouette.

## Puffino full-art stickers

The accepted Puffino companion master and V1 sticker sheet were supplied as identity and layout references. The regeneration locked Puffino's deep-blue puffin identity while requiring exactly one compact golden accordion mounted horizontally across a clearly visible rounded torso, held at two blue end plates. It explicitly prohibited vertical, dangling, beard-like, skirt-like, rib-like, floating, or below-body bellows. The three poses remained celebrate, encourage, and connect on a transparent landscape canvas.

## Cymbi companion, habitat, and full-art stickers

Each accepted Cymbi format was supplied as an identity or edit reference. The regenerations moved the paired golden cymbals completely off the chest and belly and reinterpreted them as dorsal ladybug wing covers. The habitat sprite and companion now keep a plain red front torso with the gold shell visible behind the body. The stickers open the back wings for celebrate, fold them along the back for encourage, and leave one slightly open behind the shoulder for connect. Every prompt explicitly prohibited chest discs, belly plates, breast-like or bra-like arrangements, front-mounted cymbals, and human anatomy.

## Spirlo companion, habitat, and stickers

Each existing Spirlo format was supplied as its own edit target. All three prompts changed only eye anatomy: exactly two eyes total, both at the ends of the two upright stalks, with no eyes or eye-like marks on the cream face. The friendly mouth, amber snail anatomy, low foot, and integrated coiled French-horn shell were locked. The companion retained its neutral pixel pose, the habitat source retained its 32×32-ready roaming pose, and the sticker sheet retained celebrate, encourage, and connect gestures.

## Plinka companion, habitat, and stickers

Each existing Plinka format was supplied as its own identity-preserving edit target. The attached kalimba rack and underlying ordinary quills were replaced by one integrated fan of softly rounded metallic tine-spines growing directly from the back. Graded lengths and longitudinal highlights make the spines playable while the fan itself preserves the hedgehog silhouette. Every prompt prohibited a bridge, crossbar, frame, screws, straps, separate instrument, double-layered quills, or sharp needle treatment.
