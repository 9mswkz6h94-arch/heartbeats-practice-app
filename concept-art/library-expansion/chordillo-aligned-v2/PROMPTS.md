# Musical Zoo — Chordillo-aligned V2 prompt record

These are app-local concept prompts for the Heart Beats Practice App. They do not establish the canonical Rainbow Heart illustration language and do not replace the V1 sprite library.

## Shared visual direction

Create one original Musical Zoo companion as a polished late-1990s color-handheld-game pixel-art character. Match the finish and creature appeal of the purple Chordillo reference: carefully hand-placed medium-resolution pixels, dark plum-near-black stepped outline, rounded compact anatomy, large friendly readable eyes, subtle cheek and body volume, a disciplined 6–10 tone character palette, soft highlight clusters, gentle shadow clusters, and tiny material details that remain clear when reduced. The instrument must feel naturally integrated into the animal’s anatomy, not worn like a costume. Friendly, emotionally warm, slightly magical, child-safe, and collectible; cute rather than babyish. Full body, neutral three-quarter pose, centered with generous breathing room. Genuine transparent background with clean hard pixel edges and no halo. No floor, scenery, egg, musical notes, text, label, frame, UI, watermark, extra characters, or loose instrument props. Original design only; do not imitate a named game franchise. Preserve the supplied identity reference while upgrading its rendering quality; do not redesign the animal/instrument pairing.

## Riffin

Use case: identity-preserve / style-transfer. Input 1 is the starter-trio sheet and contains both the Riffin identity at left and the Chordillo quality anchor at right. Create Riffin alone: an orange fox whose rounded acoustic-guitar resonator is naturally his cream belly, with exactly three dark strings; his long orange tail becomes a wooden guitar neck and ends in exactly three tuning pegs; oversized triangular ears, cream muzzle and inner ears, dark paws, warm open smile, alert but kind eyes. No separate guitar and no egg. Apply the shared visual direction.
