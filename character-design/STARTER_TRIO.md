## Chordillo's hybrid chord shell

Chordillo's armor bands form an original musical surface that suggests both keyboard groupings and tuned percussion bars. It should read as a naturally integrated **chord shell**, not a piano, xylophone, or instrument carried on the back. Playing several neighboring bands together visually represents harmony.

## Parallel-work reconciliation

- `tempo` is a historical draft ID for the rhythm frog. It is not a fourth starter and must not become a production character ID. Its useful writing and assets may be adapted into `boppo` with provenance recorded.
- `lyra` contains multiple historical attempts. The owl is not a starter. The later armadillo placeholders may inform Chordillo, but `lyra` is not the production ID for the harmony starter.
- `ringlet` is not a starter. Its rabbit + hand-bell listening concept is preserved in `IDEA_VAULT.md`.
- Do not delete or broadly rename these draft folders while concurrent or uncommitted work may depend on them. Canonical packets should be created deliberately, then old drafts archived only after human approval.

## Preservation boundary

The starter trio adds new inventory. Existing pets remain Founding Friends in Legacy Grove with all ownership, XP, stage, duplicates, merge stages, and dates intact.

## Approval boundary

"Canonical identity" does not mean "final artwork." Each character still passes the six review gates in `README.md`. No starter enters app code until its manifest, assets, voice pack, and accessibility copy are explicitly accepted.
