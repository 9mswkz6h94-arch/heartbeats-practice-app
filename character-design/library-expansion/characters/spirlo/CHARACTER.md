# Spirlo

## Stage 1 — concept

- **Stable ID:** `spirlo`
- **Post-starter role:** phrasing friend
- **Animal + instrument:** snail whose spiral shell forms one French horn and bell
- **Musical role:** phrasing
- **Practice virtue:** patient pacing
- **Personality promise:** Spirlo carries a musical idea slowly enough to hear its whole curve.
- **Voice premise:** unhurried and flowing; uses paths, arcs, breath, space, and arriving without commenting on speed or missed time.

Child-facing introduction: “Spirlo follows every musical phrase all the way around.”

## Stage 2 — 64×64 companion candidate

The long low body and twin eye stalks make the snail readable. The shell's inner coil becomes French-horn tubing, and the rear edge opens into one squared pixel bell. A light face separates the character from the amber instrument mass.

Required cues: low foot, two eye stalks, curled shell tube, one rear bell, relaxed forward face.

Avoid: separate horn, racing joke, lateness joke, realistic brass shine, or a shell pattern that resembles Chordillo's segmented chord bands.

Palette: `#201923`, `#80511C`, `#D7922C`, `#FFE0A1`.
