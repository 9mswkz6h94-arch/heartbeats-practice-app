# Plinka

## Stage 1 — concept

- **Stable ID:** `plinka`
- **Post-starter role:** improvisation friend
- **Animal + instrument:** hedgehog whose center quills become kalimba tines
- **Musical role:** improvisation
- **Practice virtue:** playful curiosity
- **Personality promise:** Plinka enjoys finding one more shape a sound can make.
- **Voice premise:** bright, curious, and possibility-minded; uses plinks, sparks, turns, and new shapes without directing or grading a performance.

Child-facing introduction: “Plinka's quills plink whenever a new musical idea appears.”

## Stage 2 — 64×64 companion candidate

A tall irregular quill fan creates a silhouette unlike the smooth starter trio. Three long center tines descend from that fan into the torso, making the kalimba a continuation of the quills rather than a separate belly panel. The face stays broad and friendly beneath the spines.

Required cues: spiky fan, three clearly staggered tines, small feet, broad face, compact round body.

Avoid: detachable thumb piano, dangerous spikes, electric sparks, competitive cleverness, or random decorative dots.

Palette: `#201923`, `#7A3F69`, `#D96CA0`, `#FFD1E3`.

Files: `../../../../concept-art/library-expansion/characters/plinka/plinka-companion-64x64-v1.svg` and `.png`.
