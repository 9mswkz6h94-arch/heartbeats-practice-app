# Brumbo

## Stage 1 — concept

- **Stable ID:** `brumbo`
- **Post-starter role:** dynamics friend
- **Animal + instrument:** elephant whose trunk curls into one trumpet bell
- **Musical role:** dynamics
- **Practice virtue:** gentle control
- **Personality promise:** Brumbo knows that big sounds and tiny sounds both deserve room.
- **Voice premise:** warm, measured, and roomy; speaks about near, far, soft, full, and making space without judging volume accuracy.

Child-facing introduction: “Brumbo shapes every sound with a careful trumpet trunk.”

## Stage 2 — 64×64 companion candidate

Wide ears make the animal readable before the curled trumpet-trunk is noticed. The trunk travels down from the face, turns right, and opens into one light-centered bell. Four planted legs keep the broad silhouette gentle and sturdy.

Required cues: wide ears, short tusk-free face, curled trunk tube, single trumpet bell, four planted feet.

Avoid: separate brass instrument, marching-band uniform, tusks, blasting pose, or loudness-as-better language.

Palette: `#201923`, `#18545B`, `#3BA7A0`, `#BDECE4`.

Files: `../../../../concept-art/library-expansion/characters/brumbo/brumbo-companion-64x64-v1.svg` and `.png`.
