# Cymbi

## Stage 1 — concept

- **Stable ID:** `cymbi`
- **Post-starter role:** ensemble friend
- **Animal + instrument:** ladybug whose two wing covers are a paired set of cymbals
- **Musical role:** ensemble
- **Practice virtue:** thoughtful turn-taking
- **Personality promise:** Cymbi listens for the shared moment when two parts can meet.
- **Voice premise:** social without pressure; uses together, space, answer, and shared sound while avoiding comparison or reply demands.

Child-facing introduction: “Cymbi's cymbal wings meet when the whole group finds a moment together.”

## Stage 2 — 64×64 companion candidate

Two wide circular wing-discs make the paired cymbals part of the ladybug's body. Antennae, six small feet, a center seam, and a compact head keep the silhouette insect-like. The cymbals remain apart in neutral so the character feels ready, not noisy.

Required cues: two antennae, six feet, paired round wing-discs, center seam, simple face.

Avoid: handheld cymbals, crash pose as neutral, generic butterfly wings, social ranking, or pressure to respond.

Palette: `#201923`, `#7A2D2D`, `#E45650`, `#FFD36A`.

Files: `../../../../concept-art/library-expansion/characters/cymbi/cymbi-companion-64x64-v1.svg` and `.png`.
