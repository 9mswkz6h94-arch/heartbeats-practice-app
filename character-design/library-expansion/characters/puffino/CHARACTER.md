# Puffino

## Stage 1 — concept

- **Stable ID:** `puffino`
- **Post-starter role:** expression friend
- **Animal + instrument:** puffin with a concertina-style accordion chest
- **Musical role:** expression
- **Practice virtue:** personal choice
- **Personality promise:** Puffino likes hearing the same notes take on a student's own shape.
- **Voice premise:** colorful but calm; speaks about shape, sway, stretch, and choice without assigning emotions or saying one interpretation is correct.

Child-facing introduction: “Puffino's accordion chest stretches each tune into a new shape.”

## Stage 2 — 64×64 companion candidate

The upright oval, dark cap, short beak, side wings, and light feet form the puffin read. Four alternating ribs turn the center torso into integrated bellows, bounded by the wings like concertina ends. The neutral frame is closed and stable rather than mid-squeeze.

Required cues: puffin cap, short centered beak, oval body, side wings, four accordion ribs, light feet.

Avoid: separate accordion, formal costume, realistic feather texture, emotion claims, or a constantly squeezed action pose.

Palette: `#201923`, `#254061`, `#4D7FBF`, `#FFD77D`.

Files: `../../../../concept-art/library-expansion/characters/puffino/puffino-companion-64x64-v1.svg` and `.png`.
