# Ringlet

## Stage 1 — concept

- **Stable ID:** `ringlet`
- **Post-starter role:** early listening friend; not a starter
- **Animal + instrument:** rabbit with a hand-bell torso and one visible clapper
- **Musical role:** listening
- **Practice virtue:** careful attention
- **Personality promise:** Ringlet notices first, then answers with one calm little chime.
- **Voice premise:** attentive, warm, and concise; uses ears, chimes, quiet, and noticing as images without claiming accuracy.

Child-facing introduction: “Ringlet tips one ear toward every new sound.”

## Stage 2 — 64×64 companion candidate

The tall uneven ears create the first read. The head is wide and calm, while the lower body flares into one hand-bell skirt with a single center clapper. Compact haunches and a cottontail keep the silhouette recognizably rabbit-like rather than becoming a bell with ears.

Required cues: one ear slightly shorter/forward, one upright ear, bell skirt, one clapper, cottontail, calm face.

Avoid: handheld bell, alarmed eyes, metallic shading, extra clappers, or a generic symmetrical mascot pose.

Palette: `#201923`, `#4A3F7A`, `#8B7FD1`, `#FFE9B8`.

Files: `../../../../concept-art/library-expansion/characters/ringlet/ringlet-companion-64x64-v1.svg` and `.png`.
