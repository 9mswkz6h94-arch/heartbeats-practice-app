## Locked construction rules for this review set

1. **Canvas and edge:** 64×64 SVG and PNG, transparent background, integer coordinates, `shape-rendering="crispEdges"`, nearest-neighbor enlargement.
2. **Palette:** exactly four opaque colors—shared outline `#201923`, one dark accent, one primary, and one light accent. Transparency is the only background.
3. **No raster effects:** no antialiasing, gradients, filters, opacity, texture, blur, soft shadow, embedded image, or subpixel placement.
4. **Silhouette before detail:** the animal reads from the outer contour at native size. Interior markings may support but never rescue the animal identity.
5. **One anatomical instrument:** exactly one instrument is built into the body. It is not carried, strapped on, or accompanied by loose mallets, sticks, or props.
6. **Face-first companion:** large eyes, a small readable mouth, and a friendly stable expression occupy the upper visual third. Expressions stay curious, calm, or glad—not alarmed, ashamed, or needy.
7. **Grounded stance:** feet, haunches, or a low body give every neutral companion a stable resting pose. No idle bounce is implied by the base art.
8. **Instrument share:** the integrated instrument consumes enough of the silhouette or torso to read at native scale, generally about one quarter to two fifths of the opaque footprint.
9. **Healthy empty space:** outer forms keep visible transparent margins. Fine hardware, fur texture, feathers, and ornamental pixels are removed before enlarging the drawing.
10. **Character colors are identity, not UI semantics:** palettes distinguish creatures but do not label lesson categories, status, success, warning, or urgency. They are not individual Logo Spectrum assignments.

## Family resemblance without recoloring

The family resemblance comes from shared outline, four-color construction, face scale, grounded pose, hard pixel clusters, and integrated-instrument logic. It does not require identical body proportions. This set deliberately spans tall, wide, spiky, oval, round, and low-profile silhouettes.

Repeated facial grammar is limited to readable eyes and a small mouth. Ears, trunks, quills, wings, beaks, stalks, tails, and instrument anatomy must do the actual identity work.

## Voice premise lock

Each candidate receives only a voice premise at this gate. Future packs must remain authored and deterministic, respond to actual events, and avoid pressure, comparison, guilt, urgency, sadness about absence, streak-loss language, or unmeasured accuracy claims. No runtime voice pack is authored or approved here.

## Open visual decisions

- Whether Ringlet's bell skirt reads as an instrument without a stronger rim cue.
- Whether Brumbo's trunk bell needs a wider flare while retaining the elephant silhouette.
- Whether Plinka's body tines read as kalimba anatomy rather than generic stripes.
- Whether Puffino needs a stronger side-view beak or can remain front-facing with the accordion chest.
- Whether Cymbi's paired cymbal wings read as music before they read as ordinary ladybug wing covers.
- Whether Spirlo's French-horn spiral is distinct enough from Chordillo's chord shell when shown at Zoo scale.

These are review questions, not reasons to silently expand the palette or add detail.
