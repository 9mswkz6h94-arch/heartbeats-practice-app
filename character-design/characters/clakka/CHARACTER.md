# Clakka

## Core identity

- **One-sentence concept:** Clakka is a fiddler crab whose two front claws make one friendly castanet pair, turning small in-between beats into something visible.
- **Stable ID:** `clakka`
- **Animal:** fiddler crab
- **Integrated instrument:** castanet claws
- **Musical role:** subdivision
- **Practice virtue:** patient counting
- **Starter identity or discovery role:** Home-bound resident of Rhythm Riverbank; not a starter and not a default companion.
- **Why this character is distinct from the accepted cast:** Clakka focuses on the tiny spaces between beats, adding subdivision without replacing Boppo's broad rhythm role or Cymbi's ensemble role.

## Personality

- **Child-facing introduction:** Clakka finds the tiny steps between the big beats.
- **Personality promise:** A patient side-walking friend who makes room for small counts and quiet pauses.
- **Response voice traits:** Playful, measured, and lightly percussive; favors step, between, click, and room.
- **Voice avoids:** prompting, urgency, guilt, comparison, accuracy claims, sadness about absence

## Anatomy lock

- **Strongest silhouette anchor:** One broad fiddler claw and one smaller claw held as an unmistakable, friendly pair.
- **Anatomy invariant:** The two front claws are the only castanet mechanism, each a single hinged shell pair; no castanet is carried outside the crab body.
- **Countable features:** Two eyes on short stalks, two front castanet claws, six visible walking legs, one rounded body, one short tail fan.
- **Front/side/three-quarter visibility rules:** Front shows both eye stalks and both claw hinges; side keeps the broad claw silhouette and at least four walking legs readable; three-quarter roaming pose keeps one claw open, one claw closed, and the body grounded.
- **Must never look like:** A crab holding separate clackers, a drum player, a human hand, sharp pincers, extra claws, or a character carrying a percussion prop.
