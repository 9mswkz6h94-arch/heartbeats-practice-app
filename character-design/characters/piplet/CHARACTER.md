# Piplet

## Core identity

- **One-sentence concept:** Piplet is a sparrow whose beak is a tiny piccolo, making clear musical entrances feel friendly and possible.
- **Stable ID:** `piplet`
- **Animal:** sparrow
- **Integrated instrument:** piccolo beak
- **Musical role:** articulation
- **Practice virtue:** clear beginnings
- **Starter identity or discovery role:** Home-bound resident of Melody Meadow; not a starter and not a default companion.
- **Why this character is distinct from the accepted cast:** Piplet adds articulation—how a sound starts and stops—without duplicating Riffin's melody, Ringlet's listening, or Boppo's rhythm.

## Personality

- **Child-facing introduction:** Piplet makes every entrance feel possible.
- **Personality promise:** A tiny, bright friend who notices the shape of a beginning without judging the result.
- **Response voice traits:** Brief, bright, precise, and welcoming; uses starts, spaces, taps, and little phrases.
- **Voice avoids:** prompting, urgency, guilt, comparison, accuracy claims, sadness about absence

## Anatomy lock

- **Strongest silhouette anchor:** A teardrop beak with a short tube profile and two quick folded wings.
- **Anatomy invariant:** The single beak is the whole piccolo mechanism, with exactly three small finger holes along its lower edge; the body keeps exactly two wings and two feet.
- **Countable features:** One beak/tube, three finger holes, two eyes, two wings, two feet.
- **Front/side/three-quarter visibility rules:** Front shows both eyes and the beak's three holes; side keeps the beak's tube silhouette readable; three-quarter roaming pose keeps one wing, both feet, and the beak opening visible.
- **Must never look like:** A sparrow carrying a flute, a chest pipe, a second beak, a horn, a dangling tube, or a sharp weapon-like beak.
